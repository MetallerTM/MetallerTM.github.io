_T1T2ne_ is an helper software package to setup protein dynamics <sup>15</sup>N T<sub>1</sub> and T<sub>2</sub> and hetNOE experiments.
The idea is to save spectrometer time and to optimize the quality of the data, by automatizing the selection of the parametes. 
The software is executed from command line, and the main entry point is the ``t1t2ne`` command. 

Full documentation is available at [https://t1ttune.readthedocs.io/en/latest/](https://t1ttune.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).
